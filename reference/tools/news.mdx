The `get_news` tool surfaces the latest cryptocurrency headlines from aggregated news sources, letting your AI client answer questions about market sentiment, recent project developments, regulatory updates, and breaking events — all without leaving your conversation.

## `get_news`

Fetches recent news articles matching a coin name, ticker symbol, or freeform topic keyword. Each article in the response includes a short summary and an automated sentiment score so your AI client can quickly convey market mood alongside raw headlines.

### Parameters

<ParamField body="query" type="string" required>
  The search term to match against article titles and content. You can pass a coin name (`"Bitcoin"`), a ticker symbol (`"ETH"`), or any topic keyword (`"SEC regulation"`, `"Layer 2"`). Case-insensitive.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="limit" type="number" default="5">
  The maximum number of articles to return. Accepts values between `1` and `20`. Keep this low when you only need a quick sentiment check; increase it for comprehensive research queries.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="language" type="string" default="en">
  ISO 639-1 language code for the desired article language, e.g. `"en"` for English, `"es"` for Spanish, or `"de"` for German. Availability depends on the configured news provider.
</ParamField>

### Response Schema

<ResponseField name="articles" type="object[]">
  Array of news articles ordered by publication date, newest first.

  <Expandable title="article object fields">
    <ResponseField name="articles[].title" type="string">
      The article's headline as published by the source.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="articles[].url" type="string">
      The canonical URL of the full article.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="articles[].source" type="string">
      The name of the publishing outlet, e.g. `"CoinDesk"`, `"The Block"`, `"Reuters"`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="articles[].published_at" type="string">
      ISO 8601 timestamp of when the article was published.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="articles[].summary" type="string">
      A short auto-generated or publisher-provided summary of the article content, typically 1–3 sentences.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="articles[].sentiment" type="string">
      Automated sentiment classification: `"positive"`, `"neutral"`, or `"negative"`. Based on NLP analysis of the title and summary.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

### Example Request

```json
{
  "tool": "get_news",
  "input": {
    "query": "Ethereum",
    "limit": 3,
    "language": "en"
  }
}
```

## Example Prompts

You can ask your AI client questions like the following, and it will call `get_news` automatically to find relevant articles:

- *"What's the latest news on Ethereum?"*
- *"Any negative news about Solana today?"*
- *"Summarise recent headlines about Bitcoin ETF approvals."*
- *"Has there been any regulatory news affecting DeFi this week?"*
- *"What are people saying about the Uniswap v4 launch?"*

<Info>
  CryptoMCP aggregates headlines from a curated list of crypto and financial news sources including CoinDesk, The Block, Decrypt, CoinTelegraph, and major financial wire services. The news index is refreshed every **15 minutes**. Articles older than 7 days are not returned by default.
</Info>

<Note>
  Sentiment analysis is performed automatically using NLP models and may not always reflect the true tone of a complex article. Treat `sentiment` as a quick signal rather than a definitive judgment — always read the full article before acting on its content.
</Note>
